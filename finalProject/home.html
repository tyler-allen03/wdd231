Site Overview

Created by Scott Cawthon in 2014, Five Nights at Freddy's (FNaF) is an indie survival horror franchise that evolved from a simple point-and-click PC game into a massive multimedia universe featuring games, books, and a feature film. Players typically assume the role of a night-shift security guard at a family pizza restaurant, using limited resources like security cameras and doors to survive six-hour shifts against hostile, haunted animatronics. The series' dark overarching narrative follows the child victims of co-founder William Afton, who possess the animatronics, with complex lore cryptically hidden across 8-bit minigames, secret files, and environmental details.

There will also be a form at the bottom of the encyclopedia to inform me of any characters that I might have missed.

Character Exclusions

This encyclopedia will feature most characters within the FNaF franchise, however many characters are not really relevant to the lore. Those characters will not be shown here on this encyclopedia. I will not be showing characters such as the twisted animatronics, most of the Special Delivery animatronic skins, any character only found in FNaF World, and any character only found in Fazbear Frieghts and Tales from the PizzaPlex.
